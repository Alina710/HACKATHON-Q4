Welcome to the *ROS 2 Nervous System* course for humanoid robotics!

In this course, we will explore how *ROS 2 (Robot Operating System 2)* serves as the central nervous system of a humanoid robot. ROS 2 is an open-source framework designed to manage the communication and coordination of various components of robotic systems. It allows robots to interact with their environment, process data, and perform complex tasks, all while maintaining modularity and flexibility.

### *What is ROS 2?*

ROS 2 is the next generation of the popular *Robot Operating System (ROS)*, built to address the limitations of the original system while expanding its capabilities for modern robotics. Unlike its predecessor, ROS 2 is designed with support for real-time operations, security, and multi-robot communication, making it ideal for humanoid robots.

Just like the nervous system in the human body, ROS 2 acts as the communication backbone, ensuring all parts of the robot—sensors, actuators, and control systems—work together seamlessly. ROS 2 is built on a *distributed architecture*, where different parts of the robot can operate independently yet communicate with each other in real-time.

### *Why ROS 2 is Crucial for Humanoid Robots*

Humanoid robots are designed to mimic human behavior, which requires advanced capabilities like:

* *Perception*: Understanding the environment through sensors like cameras, LiDAR, and depth sensors.
* *Decision-making*: Processing the data to make real-time decisions, such as navigating obstacles or picking up objects.
* *Actuation*: Performing human-like motions using a combination of motors, servos, and other actuators.
